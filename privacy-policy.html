Euthenia

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

In short: Euthenia keeps everything you enter on your own phone. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet. We, the developer, never receive your data.

Who we are

Euthenia is an Android app made by Euna Magpantay (“I”, “we”, “us”), an individual developer in the Philippines, as a personal hobby project. It is not published by a company. It is a wellbeing tracker for you and, if you choose, your family: money, food, travel, health, reminders and private notes.

Questions about this policy can be sent to eugenio.magpantay@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores only what you type into it. All of it is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Money: money in and out, categories, investments and the currency you choose.
  • Food and travel: meals, drinks, journeys and any costs you add.
  • Health: sleep, exercise, health notes, blood pressure, blood sugar, medicines, treatment schedules, doses taken, screen time and things to take care of.
  • Reminders: titles, times, places and details of the reminders you create.
  • People: the names you give to family members you choose to track.
  • Notes: private notes, which are encrypted on your phone with a password only you know.
  • Settings: such as light or dark theme.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone.

If you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example, about your download and device). We do not control that. If you email us, we use your email only to reply.

Health information

Health entries are treated like everything else: they stay on your phone and are never sent to us. Euthenia is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice and is not a substitute for a doctor.

Private notes

Notes are encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM) using a key made from your password. Your password is never stored. If you forget it, the notes cannot be recovered, by you or by us. If you switch on fingerprint unlock, the check is done by Android; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint.

Backups

Euthenia is excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and from phone-to-phone transfer, so your data is not copied to Google’s servers by the app.

You can make your own backup from Settings → Backup. This saves one file to a place you choose. You can protect the file with a password, which encrypts all of it; without a password, only the notes inside it are encrypted. The file is yours: who can read it depends on where you keep it and who you give it to. If you save it to a cloud drive, that provider’s privacy policy applies to the file.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or the internet. Backup files are opened and saved through Android’s own file picker, so the app only sees the file you pick.

Notifications

Reminders can show names and details, such as a medicine and its dose, on your screen. You can switch on “Hide reminder details” in Settings so that notifications only say what kind of reminder they are.

Information about other people and children

If you track family members, including children, the information about them is entered by you and stays on your phone like the rest. Please only record information about other people that you are entitled to keep. Euthenia is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can:

  • delete any entry inside the app;
  • erase everything by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app;
  • delete any backup files you have saved, wherever you keep them.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and we cannot give you a copy of it — use Settings → Backup → Export instead.

Security

Data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read on a phone that has not been modified. Notes have their own encryption on top of that. While notes are unlocked, the app blocks screenshots and screen recording. The rest of your data is as safe as your phone, so we recommend using a screen lock.

Your rights

Data protection laws, such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and the GDPR, give you rights over your personal data. Because all of your data is on your phone and under your control, you can exercise them directly in the app. If you have a concern, you can contact us or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
